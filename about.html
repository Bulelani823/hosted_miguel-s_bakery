MIGUEL’S BAKERY & TAKE-AWAY was founded in 1992 and is proud of many years experience in baking & catering. The owner, Steve Green is dedicated to providing customers with superior quality products and exceptional service, “a place where everybody knows your name.” Miguel’s Bakery success is due to the loyalty of our customers and dedication of our employees. All our bakery products are always freshly baked daily from the best ingredients available using traditional & unique recipes & techniques. Some of our ingredients include pure fresh cream, rich chocolate, whole eggs, fresh fruit, nuts and creamy butter. We offer a variety of delicious gateau’s, fresh cream or butter cream slices, tarts, desserts, doughnuts, biscuits, muffins, special breads, fancy rolls, pastries, custom made wedding and birthday cakes and other tasty treats. Our take-away products are also freshly prepared and has a variety of products which includes burgers, fries, sandwiches, fish & chips, curries, fried/grilled chicken and the special’s of the day. Our Bakery is able to supply any need and enjoys the challenges of custom designed cakes, deserts and fancy breads. We service catering companies, schools, hospitals, hotels, restaurants, weddings and special engagements. No occasion is too big or too small for us to handle. Miguel’s Bakery is well known in the Pietermaritzburg area for producing great tasting and great looking products which attracts customers from throughout the KwaZulu-Natal (KZN) Province. It is becoming the most sought after bakery in KZN. We have a team of trained bakers and confectioners who continuously strives at producing creative products of the finest quality and providing excellent service. The warm service, and mouth watering products are the soul of Miguel’s Bakery. We pride ourselves on presentation and taste and guarantee that your experience would be soul satisfying.

Our mission is to be able to promote our sweet treats to the general public and making them afford for everyone to be able to get their hands on high quality goods. We take proud in making sure customers enjoy their sweet treats.
